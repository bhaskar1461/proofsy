## 🌟 Executive Overview
Proofsy is an institutional-grade, zero-trust digital credentialing platform that allows organizations to design, issue, manage, and cryptographically verify tamper-proof digital certificates at scale. 

By combining **asymmetric 2048-bit RSA cryptography** with **natural-language AI generation** and **whitelabel branding**, Proofsy transforms static, forgeable PDF diplomas into secure, verifiable web credentials that amplify institutional prestige and drive organic employer traffic.

### 1. Zero-Trust Cryptographic Assurance
* **Mathematical Authenticity**: Every credential is cryptographically signed using the institution's private key.
* **Offline Verification**: Certificates can be verified mathematically on any device without contacting a centralized database.
* **Tamper Invalidation**: Modifying even a single character in the certificate name, date, or grade immediately breaks the mathematical signature.

### 2. AI-Powered Visual Template Studio
* **Prompt-to-Certificate**: Create executive diplomas, tech hackathon awards, or medical certifications using conversational AI prompts.
* **Instant Inspiration Cloner**: Upload or paste (`Ctrl+V`) an existing certificate image to automatically extract dimensions and smart data fields.
* **Vector Quality**: Exports crisp, print-ready vector PDFs, high-resolution PNGs, and scalable web SVGs.

### 3. Ask Proofsy — Natural Language AI Operator
* Powered by enterprise Llama 3.3 70B AI.
* Organizers can ask: *"Find all certificates issued this week"*, *"Duplicate template 'Gold Diploma' for Cohort 8"*, or *"Generate a summary of total credentials issued"*.

### 4. Institutional Compliance & Audit Trail
* **Automated Key Lifecycle**: 1-click zero-downtime key rotation with continuous backwards compatibility.
* **Compliance Packages**: Instant 1-click export of cryptographically timestamped SOC-2 and ISO-27001 audit JSON files.

### 5. Social Virality & Brand Exposure
* **1-Click LinkedIn Integration**: Direct link to populate LinkedIn Licenses & Certifications.
* **Embeddable GitHub Badges**: Live SVG badges for recipient portfolios and developer READMEs.
* **Unified Student Transcripts**: Clean, searchable public recipient profiles at `proofsy.tech/recipient/[email]`.
