## 🎯 Executive Overview

As Edwisly powers AI-driven Learning Management Systems (LMS), Outcome-Based Education (OBE), and NBA/NAAC accreditation across engineering colleges and universities in India, **cryptographically verifiable digital credentials** are a high-demand add-on for institutional clients.

Currently, over 90% of Indian institutions issue static PDF certificates that can be easily forged in graphic editing software. By partnering with **Proofsy**, Edwisly can immediately offer an **institutional-grade, tamper-proof certification suite** branded entirely under Edwisly (`credentials.edwisly.com`).

## ⚡ Core Value for Edwisly & Partner Colleges

1. **NAAC & NBA Audit-Ready Compliance**: Every certificate includes an immutable cryptographic signature and timestamped audit ledger (`proofsy.tech/compliance`), streamlining institutional accreditation reporting.
2. **Instant Zero-Trust Verification**: Recruiters and universities can verify credentials in <100ms with a smartphone camera or link — even completely offline.
3. **AI-Powered Visual Studio**: Natural-language template generation and image cloner (`proofsy.tech/designer`) allowing colleges to upload their existing diploma design and generate smart vector fields in seconds.
4. **Viral Student Portfolios**: 1-click "Add to LinkedIn Certifications" and embeddable GitHub badges drive continuous brand visibility and recruiter traffic back to Edwisly.
